THE PRODUCT HYPOTHESIS

Make the evidence easier to interpret

The opportunity to test is not whether Angel One has fund data. It is whether investors can interpret performance behaviour and compare schemes on a consistent, transparent basis within one research flow.

01

Consistency, not one endpoint

Rolling-return distributions show how outcomes changed across historical investment start dates, instead of relying on a single trailing-return window.

02

Risk with context

Explain volatility, drawdown and risk-adjusted measures with the formula, observation period, risk-free assumption and limitations visible.

03

Flexible fund comparison

Compare up to three schemes side by side using NAV-based returns, risk measures and each scheme’s available disclosures. Missing benchmark data is shown as unavailable, not guessed.

04

Honest data states

Show source dates and assumptions. When a matched benchmark series or disclosure is unavailable, say so instead of substituting or estimating it.

This is a product hypothesis to validate with investors and Angel One product data, not a claim that every element is missing from Angel One's current experience.

Metric definitions and calculation notes

Open each metric to inspect the calculation and its interpretation. Numbers are not standalone fund ratings.

Sharpe ratio

Annualised mean daily scheme return less the daily risk-free assumption, divided by daily standard deviation, then multiplied by √252. Higher is not automatically better across different categories or periods.

Sortino ratio

Annualised mean excess return divided by downside deviation. Downside deviation uses daily returns below the configured target rate. The target assumption must be disclosed.

Alpha and Beta

Beta is covariance of matched weekly scheme and benchmark returns divided by weekly benchmark variance. Alpha is the annualised weekly CAPM intercept after the configured weekly risk-free assumption. Results depend on benchmark and sample period.

Information Ratio

When available, FundLens displays the official AMFI-reported Information Ratio for the selected scheme plan and reporting period. Separately, when matched weekly TRI-derived observations are available, FundLens can calculate a research estimate as mean weekly arithmetic active return divided by the sample standard deviation of weekly active returns, multiplied by √52. The research estimate is not interchangeable with the official AMFI figure.

Tracking error and difference

Tracking error is annualised volatility of weekly active returns, using √52. Tracking difference is scheme performance less benchmark performance over the same matched period. These are central for index funds.

Rolling returns

Each eligible NAV date becomes a start date. The engine finds the first available observation on or after the holding-window target date. Adjacent windows overlap and are not independent observations.

Annualisation uses 252 trading days for NAV-only volatility, Sharpe and Sortino. Weekly benchmark-relative Alpha, tracking error and FundLens IR estimates use 52 periods per year. The current RBI 10-year G-Sec yield is used as a constant proxy, not as a daily maturity-matched risk-free curve. Official AMFI IR is displayed separately when supplied. Weekly benchmark observations are exact-date matched to NAV; there is no forward-fill. Formula choice does not generate benchmark observations: weekly Alpha, Beta, tracking error, FundLens IR and benchmark-relative rolling outcomes remain unavailable until the mapped weekly TRI-derived series is retrieved.

Methodology. For every eligible NAV date, the engine finds the first available NAV on or after the date shifted by the selected number of calendar months. It computes the holding-period return as end NAV ÷ start NAV − 1. CAGR mode annualises that return over the selected calendar window. Daily, weekly and monthly frequencies change the spacing of start dates, not the return endpoints. Overlapping windows are not independent observations.
Benchmark data limitation. FundLens requests a published weekly TRI-derived series for supported Nifty benchmarks. Benchmark-relative rolling outcomes require validated weekly observations and exact-date NAV matches. Until that data is available, the comparison is withheld. No manual upload is required or supported, and a price-return series is never substituted.
