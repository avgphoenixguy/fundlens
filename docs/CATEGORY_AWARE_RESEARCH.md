# FundLens category-aware research framework

| Category | Primary evaluation questions | Core measures |
|---|---|---|
| Active equity | Has the scheme delivered consistent returns relative to its appropriate Tier 1 benchmark, and what risk did it take? | Rolling returns, positive-window share, Sharpe, Sortino, Alpha, Beta, Information Ratio, tracking error, drawdown, TER |
| Index / passive | How closely and cheaply has the scheme replicated its benchmark? | Tracking difference, tracking error, Direct-plan TER, AUM, portfolio replication, cash / derivatives exposure |
| Debt | What credit, duration, liquidity and reinvestment risks are present? | Credit quality, YTM, modified duration, average maturity, concentration, liquidity, TER |
| Hybrid | What are the equity and debt sleeves contributing, and how are they exposed? | Asset allocation, equity benchmark and risk measures, debt credit quality and duration, rebalancing, drawdown |

FundLens does not apply a universal numeric fund score. Equity ratios are not blindly applied to debt or hybrid funds. Metrics are shown only where required inputs and history are available. Missing inputs are called out rather than imputed.
